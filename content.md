## Section 4: FAQ
Headline: Questions

- What is AEO? Answer Engine Optimization. It is the work of getting your business named when someone asks an AI assistant a direct question, like who to hire for a job in their area. Search used to return ten links. AI returns one answer with a few names in it.
- What is GEO? Generative Engine Optimization. The same goal applied to tools that write answers, like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. These tools pick businesses whose information is clear, consistent and backed by other sources.
- How is this different from SEO? SEO aims for a spot in a list of links. AEO and GEO aim for a mention inside the answer itself. Good SEO helps, but AI tools also weigh structured data, plain answers to specific questions, and what other sites say about you.
- What does the free audit include? We ask the major AI assistants the questions your customers ask, and record who gets named. We review your site and listings for what AI can and cannot read. We sit down with you on your main goals and map where AI agents could work 24/7. You get a written report either way.
- Do AI agents replace my staff? No. Agents take the repetitive work: intake, follow-up, scheduling, updating records. Your people keep the relationships and the decisions. Nothing goes out to a customer without a person approving it.
- How long until we show up in AI answers? It depends on your market and how much is already published about you. The audit gives you a baseline, and we re-test the same questions every month so you can see the change yourself.
