Questions

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization. It is the work of getting your company named when someone asks an AI assistant a direct question, like who buys mineral rights in Reeves County. Search used to return ten links. AI returns one answer with a few names in it.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization. It is the same goal applied to tools that write answers, like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. These tools pick companies whose information is clear, consistent and backed by other sources.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO aims for a spot in a list of links. AEO and GEO aim for a mention inside the answer itself. Good SEO helps, but AI tools also weigh structured data, plain answers to specific questions, and what other sites say about you.

What does the free audit include?

Do AI agents replace my staff?

No. Agents take the repetitive work: intake, follow-up, chasing documents, updating records. Your people keep the relationships and the decisions. Nothing goes out to an owner or partner without a person approving it.

How long until we show up in AI answers?

It depends on your market and how much is already published about you. The audit gives you a baseline, and we re-test the same questions every month so you can see the change yourself.
