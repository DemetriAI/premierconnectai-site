AEO and GEO: get your business named in AI answers

PremierConnect AI · Austin, Texas · working with businesses anywhere

Your customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity who to call. Search gave them ten links. AI gives them one answer with a few names in it. AEO and GEO is the work of making sure one of those names is yours.

What we do, in order

  1. Baseline audit. We ask the four major AI assistants the questions your buyers ask and record who gets named. You see it before you pay anything.
  2. Make the site readable. AI crawlers allowed, main text in the page HTML, business markup with your real name and contact details, a sitemap, and an llms.txt file. Usually about an hour.
  3. Answer the questions you are missing. A plain answer to each missed question, in the question's own words, with the service and city named. Price questions get a real answer, because "call for a quote" never gets quoted.
  4. Same identity everywhere. Your name, address and phone match on your site, Google, and every directory. Mismatches make AI unsure you are one business.
  5. Be where AI looks. The audit shows which sites the assistants quoted. We get you listed or mentioned there first.

How we measure it

The same questions, asked to the same four assistants, two to four weeks after the fixes go live. You see who named you, at what position, and which sources they used. We count what moved and do not round up. Whatever is still missed becomes the next round.

Questions

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization. It is the work of getting your business named when someone asks an AI assistant a direct question, like who to hire for a job in their area.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization. The same goal applied to tools that write answers, like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Who does AEO and GEO work for businesses in Austin, Texas?

PremierConnect AI, based in Austin. We test how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity answer your customers' questions, fix what AI cannot read or find about you, and re-test the same questions every month. We work with businesses outside Austin too.

How long does AEO take to work?

Site fixes can be checked the same day. Being named in AI answers takes longer, because the assistants answer from search indexes and new pages take two to four weeks to get crawled. We re-test at that point with the same questions.

Can you guarantee my business shows up first in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honest can. No one controls what an AI assistant says. We show you a baseline before you pay and count what moved after each round of fixes.
