A mineral owner gets an offer in the mail. Before calling anyone back, they can now ask ChatGPT who buys mineral rights in their county and whether the offer is fair. An operator can ask it who does reserves reports. The answer has a few names in it. Our job is to make one of them yours.

Who this is for

  • Mineral and royalty buyers who want owners to find them by county.
  • Petroleum consultants and reserves engineers who want to be found for the work they want.
  • Small oil and gas firms that run on referrals and have never checked what AI says about them.

Why this market is different

  • The questions are local. Nobody asks for the best mineral buyer in America. They ask who buys in Midland County. A general page does not answer that.
  • AI reads more than your site. In the mineral market audits we have run, the assistants built their answers from industry registries, buyer directories and other firms' own pages.
  • AI can file you under the wrong work. A reserves firm can get labeled a mineral buyer. That sends you the wrong calls and costs you the right ones.
  • Public reviews are not the only path. Many firms here would rather not have them, and the plan does not depend on them.

What we do, in order

  1. Baseline on your market. Eight questions built on your counties and services, asked to four AI assistants. You see who gets named before you pay.
  2. Make your site readable. AI crawlers allowed, business markup, a sitemap and an llms.txt file. Behind Cloudflare, this goes in with no visible change to your site.
  3. Answer what you are missing. A plain answer for each missed question, by county, basin or service. You approve every word.
  4. Same identity everywhere. One name, one address and one phone number across your site and every listing.
  5. Get listed where AI looks. The audit shows which registries and directories the assistants quote. You approve each listing.
  6. Re-test. The same questions, two to four weeks later. We count what moved and do not round up.
